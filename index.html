JOIN LGY

Interested in competing or creating content with Legacy? Here's how to get started.

How to apply

Contact us

Join our Discord or send a message through TikTok.

Introduce yourself

Tell us your username, games, platform, and whether you're applying as a player or creator.

Wait for review

Management will review your request and contact you about next steps.

After acceptance

You may announce your membership publicly if you're comfortable. Posting an announcement video is encouraged, not required.

What you can apply for

Competitive player · YouTube creator · Twitch streamer · TikTok creator · Video editor

Application details

Have your gaming username, preferred division, platform, and a public creator link ready if you have one. No minimum rank or follower count is required.
