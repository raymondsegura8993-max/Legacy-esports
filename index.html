JOIN LGY

Interested in competing or creating content with Legacy? Here's how to get started.

How to apply

Contact us

Join our Discord or send a message through TikTok.

Introduce yourself

Tell us your username, games, platform, and whether you're applying as a player or creator.

Wait for review

Management will review your request and contact you about next steps.

After acceptance

You may announce your membership publicly if you're comfortable. Posting an announcement video is encouraged, not required.

What you can apply for

Competitive player · YouTube creator · Twitch streamer · TikTok creator · Video editor

Apply your way

No minimum rank or follower count is required. Choose how you want to contact Legacy.

TEAM STANDARDS

We want members who help make the community better.

Any rank
All skill levels may apply.
All platforms
PC, PlayStation, and Xbox players are welcome.
Respect
Treat teammates, opponents, and staff respectfully.
Fair play
No cheating, exploiting, or sabotaging teammates.
Communication
Keep in touch with your division and management.
Privacy & safety
Never share passwords, home addresses, or private information in public channels.
